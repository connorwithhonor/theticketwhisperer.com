Is this legal advice? +
No. This guide provides general educational information based on the author's 20 years of experience as a law enforcement officer. It is not legal advice and does not create an attorney-client relationship. For legal advice specific to your situation, consult a licensed California attorney.
Does this work for out-of-state tickets? +
NO. This guide is exclusively for traffic citations issued in the State of California. Laws vary significantly in other states. However, if you live out of state but received a ticket IN California, yes, this guide will help you fight it without returning to CA.
Do you offer refunds? +

What is "Trial by Written Declaration"? +
It is a procedure allowed under California Vehicle Code 40902 that allows you to contest a citation in writing, by mail, without going to court. If the officer fails to respond (which happens often), the case is dismissed. If you are found guilty, you can STILL request a new trial in person (De Novo). It's a "no-lose" first step.
